About Me
I am a civil engineering professional with experience in construction drawings, quantity takeoff, technical documentation, and construction management support. My work combines engineering practice with an interest in artificial intelligence and computer vision for infrastructure inspection.
My research focuses on automated concrete crack segmentation and image-based geometric characterization using deep learning, with particular emphasis on progressive Mask R-CNN workflows.

An integrated mask R-CNN framework for concrete crack segmentation and skeleton-based quantification in automated infrastructure inspection.
Journal of Building Pathology and Rehabilitation, 11, Article 228. Springer Nature.
Read the published paper · View the research repository

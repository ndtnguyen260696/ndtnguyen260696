## Featured International Publication

### An integrated mask R-CNN framework for concrete crack segmentation and skeleton-based quantification in automated infrastructure inspection

**Authors:** Dat-Thanh Nguyen, Nhat-Quang Tran, Duc-Hoc Tran

**Journal:** Journal of Building Pathology and Rehabilitation

**Publisher:** Springer Nature

**Publication:** Volume 11, Article 228 (2026)

**DOI:** [10.1007/s41024-026-00900-z](https://doi.org/10.1007/s41024-026-00900-z)

This research introduces a progressive Mask R-CNN framework integrating instance-aware concrete crack segmentation, boundary and topology refinement, and skeleton-based geometric characterization.

The proposed M0–M4 framework supports automated identification of concrete crack regions and extraction of image-domain geometric information for infrastructure inspection applications.

**Research areas:**
- Concrete crack detection and instance segmentation
- Mask R-CNN and ResNet-50-FPN
- PointRend boundary refinement
- Morphological mask refinement
- Skeleton-based geometric characterization
- AI-assisted infrastructure inspection
